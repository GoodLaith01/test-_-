How does AC become DC? ⚡ Watch the current, step by step.

1️⃣ AC supply: the current changes direction 50 times a second (slowed down here so you can follow it)
2️⃣ Transformer: 230 V down to 12 V (peak ≈ 17 V)
3️⃣ Bridge rectifier: when A is +, D1 and D3 conduct. When B is +, D2 and D4 conduct. Either way, current goes through the load in the same direction.
4️⃣ Capacitor: charges at every peak and feeds the load in between, so the output becomes smooth DC

Listen to the music too. The bass switches back and forth like AC, then holds one steady note when the output becomes DC.

Vpk = √2 × Vrms
Vdc ≈ Vpk − 1.4 V
Vripple ≈ I / (2 f C)
